xkcd-script-subset.ttf is the xkcd Script font from the IPython xkcd-font project
(https://github.com/ipython/xkcd-font, xkcd-script/font/xkcd-script.ttf), cut down with pyftsubset to Latin-1 and a
few punctuation marks. It's lettering from xkcd by Randall Munroe, made into a font by the project's contributors.

Only the xkcd_panel transition uses it.
